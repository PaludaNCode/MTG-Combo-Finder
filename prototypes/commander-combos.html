Prototype — combos with your commander. Chatterfang, Squirrel General, and the 11 of 233 combos in that deck which name him. Measurements and the argument are in prototypes/commander-combos.md. C is the recommendation. Nothing here is wired to the live renderers.

A — a Commander pin

True, and inert. It names the card without saying why a deck builder should care, and the row's number is unchanged — so a combo you assemble by drawing one card still reads as costing two.

Chatterfang, Squirrel General + Pitiless Plunderer
2-card Commander
Ghave, Guru of Spores + Ashnod's Altar + Chatterfang, Squirrel General
3-card Commander

B — the size pill becomes what it costs you

The honest number, in the wrong place. A combo's size is true for everybody; cards you still have to draw is true only for this deck. Merging them makes a pill that is summed and counted across panels into a deck-dependent figure, and the size breakdown under a suggestion has to add up to the total beside it.

Chatterfang, Squirrel General + Pitiless Plunderer
draw 1
Ghave, Guru of Spores + Ashnod's Altar + Chatterfang, Squirrel General
draw 2

C — both facts, kept apart (recommended)

The size pill stays what it has always been. The new chip appears only on the 5% of rows where the deck changes the answer, and says the thing a builder acts on. On the other 222 rows nothing is added.

Chatterfang, Squirrel General + Pitiless Plunderer
2-card 1 to draw
Ghave, Guru of Spores + Ashnod's Altar + Chatterfang, Squirrel General
3-card 2 to draw
Basalt Monolith + Rings of Brighthearth
2-card — no commander in it, so nothing is added

D — C, plus the commander marked in the heading

The clearest, and the most new surface. Worth it only if C reads as unattributed — a chip saying 2 to draw beside a 3-card combo invites the question which one is free?, and this answers it. A word rather than a crown: the mana pips are drawn here and checked for carrying no text, and an icon with no text alternative beside a card name is the opposite of that rule.
