The alternatives row is where this shape gets awkward. Cleric Class has three interchangeable partners and the panel is telling you to buy one of four cards. A Buy link on the heading card alone quietly recommends the most expensive one as often as the cheapest, and the page has no price data to know which — so the link is on the row that was ranked first for combo count, and nothing on screen says that is not a purchasing recommendation. A basket does not have this problem, because by the time a card is in it the reader has picked which of the four.
