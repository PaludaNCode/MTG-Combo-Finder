MTG Combo Finder

Prototype — a filter bar over the real Chatterfang deck: 233 combos, 40 cards carrying them, measured 13 Aug 2026.

Commander declared. The deck says Chatterfang, Squirrel General is its commander, so the chip is on the bar.

Show only

Where the bar goes. Above the first results panel and outside it, because it governs three: the combos, the map beneath them, and the unofficial rows. Inside Combos in your deck it would read as that panel’s own control, and the map changing underneath would look like a bug.

What is not settled. Whether the bar should survive a new search. Keeping it means pasting a fresh deck and being told it has 9 combos; clearing it means losing the filter every time you press + Add to deck, which re-runs the search. Neither is obviously right, and the shipped answer probably has to distinguish the two.

Not filtered: the suggestions. “Which card unlocks the most wins” is a real question and a separate feature — that panel has tabs of its own, and these chips would become filters wrapping tabs.
